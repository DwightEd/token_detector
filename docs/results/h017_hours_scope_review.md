Conceptually identical transformations must not alter tokens. No prefix reset, answer repair, invented QA or alternative continuation is involved.

Global A/B counts and total characters match across the pair, but internal character positions shift. Actual BPE token lengths, masks, absolute positions and post-RoPE axes have not been checked. The Mon-Sat B subclaim remains unsupported. This tests position/nearest-day/majority explanations without pretending that every changed source changes the claim's support. No such control was materialized here.

First divergent minute55 is distinct from end-hour57, which already follows a forced minute choice. Predeclare their roles and aggregate before values; do not tune a preferred receiver, head or layer after observing results.

Changing the Sunday source field can still have direct effects through recency or retrieval. Report this limitation and the whole response unchanged; do not call the future Sunday mismatch a second independent success.
6. **Observation identity and resource scope:** freeze tokenizer/model/backend/precision, source/prompt transformations, original receiver-to-next-token mapping, complete observation coverage, controls, stopping rule and resource budget, then obtain independent review and root check/claim. This audit does not admit a model run or a risk readout.
