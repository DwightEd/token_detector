# Independent decision review: failed-probability raw energies

Decision: conditionally admissible as a separate, explicitly posthoc descriptive audit. It must not qualify, complete or repair H013's failed probability experiment. This review used only the proposed analysis contract and the already reported engineering failure; it did not open actual raw values or construction references and did not inspect the new sixteen-query outcomes.

For two finite stored energies, the unchanged candidate_distribution function defines:

\[
w(c\mid\mathcal C)=\frac{\exp(E_c)}{\sum_{d\in\mathcal C}\exp(E_d)}.
\]

These are valid finite-set Gibbs weights, and their argmax is the argmax of the original energies. This mathematical interpretation does not require the energies to be coherent unconditional language-model sequence log probabilities. It also does not repair that missing interpretation. Retain the raw sums, their original token-level provenance and the unmodified candidate log mass, including every anomalous flag. The observed violation's small magnitude is not the justification: the same separation applies to any fully finite, structurally admitted energy records.

The useful question is narrow: which source candidate do these fixed, numerically unqualified scores rank first, and how does that operational ranking differ from the previously sealed H011 burden-zero ranking? It cannot establish a valid LM probability, calibrated factuality confidence, successful semantic mechanism, natural generalization, or a promoted method. Atomic scope and forward shapes changed together, so even a ranking difference cannot uniquely separate semantic binding from numerical execution effects.

Hard admission boundaries:

1. Preserve H013's original failed gate, protocol, raw records, child seals and absence of successful root completion. Never append success to that run, relax its threshold, clamp mass, change a score, replace a token, or silently use a qualified-observer schema for it.
2. Create a distinct audit schema and fresh output tree naming the donor a failed/unqualified probability attempt. Before hashing payloads, admit its exact known namespace and all thirty-two child namespaces/seal keys. Bind the original plan, freeze, protocol, model/runtime/source identities, initial raw hashes and child seals, plus the retained failure record. Recheck those initial identities through final audit sealing.
3. Admit every planned completion and all sixteen questions. Check complete name-plus-EOS coverage, prefix/target/causal-row metadata, finite token statistics, exact raw sums and all model assets. Missing, changed or nonfinite records block this fixed-population audit; do not drop them or replace them with an easier subset. A new audit must state any checks it cannot reconstruct because the donor lacked root completion.
4. Use the original numbers and the exact existing candidate_distribution implementation, with no length correction, new temperature, probability clipping or score repair. Keep candidate mass and the failed probability-qualification flag adjacent to the derived weights. The weights are conditional Gibbs weights; original score-field names are preserved only as donor provenance, never newly certified as LM log probabilities.
5. Freeze protocol, current code identity, the complete sixteen-question/thirty-two-completion manifest, all derived weights/rankings, costs and every paired H011-b0 alignment before opening construction references. Fix the entire comparison and its tie/unknown treatment now. Preserve both arms, all source/event flip pairs, all cases, raw scores and anomalies regardless of outcome. Rank accuracy and paired rank/flip agreement are descriptive; any derived risk/AUROC is secondary and uncalibrated.
6. Match H011-b0 by exact source bytes, canonical event and candidate identities, and retained original-response/slot provenance, not by favorable scores. Pin and validate its existing sealed observations and reference packet. Preserve its original scores and failures; do not reinterpret it as a matched-budget or identical-message experiment.
7. Report the audit as exposed, constructed and posthoc. The toy source grammar already determines its truth, H008/H011 outcomes are known, and the atomic intervention was motivated by those results. Freezing this analysis before a formal label-file read prevents further analysis choices; it does not restore a new held-out test or make the result confirmatory.

This is not merely renaming a failed run if its claim is genuinely reduced to observed rankings and its failure remains prominent. It becomes an invalid rescue if a favorable ranking is presented as the original probability hypothesis passing, as evidence that the mass defect is harmless, or as proof that atomic decomposition fixes the semantic mechanism.

The shared-prefix trie qualification remains necessary for a coherent probability instrument. A later paired compound/atomic experiment using that same qualified instrument would address a different and stronger question. No new forwards are needed or authorized for this descriptive audit decision.
